Detrending experiments (sector 1)

Each version of the search scored on sector 1's three samples, before sector 2 was run. Variant 7a, a cubic spline whose knot spacing is chosen for each star, is the current default: it finds more planets and roughly halves the signals on stars with no SPOC detection. TCE counts are those in the search's period range.

Terms

TCE
Threshold Crossing Event: a periodic dip SPOC's search found in one sector. A detection, not a planet; many are eclipsing binaries or systematics.
TOI
TESS Object of Interest: a detection human vetters promoted to a candidate, often using several sectors. Dispositions: CP confirmed planet, KP known planet, PC planet candidate, EB eclipsing binary, V variable star, IS instrumental.
Found again
The search reports a signal at the same period (or a simple multiple or fraction of it) whose transits fall at the same times.
Too weak in one sector
Fewer than two transits in the sector, or the transit measured at its known ephemeris on the search's flux is below the detection threshold. Such a TOI was found with more data than one sector.
Samples
TOI sample: every TOI listing the sector, on a star with a 2-minute light curve there. TCE samples: 60 stars with TCEs, spread across SPOC's signal strength, plus 40 random stars with none. Sector 1 has two (development and a hold-out sharing no star with it); sector 2 has one, run after all tuning was done on sector 1.
